# Weak narrative: rushed 'and then' timeline, flat characters, dream ending

## Question

Write a story with the title, 'The ball'.

## Answer

One day there was a boy called Jake and he loved football. He played football every day with his friends at the park.

On saturday Jake went to the park with his ball. He kicked the ball really hard and it went over the fence into a old house's garden. Everyone said the house was haunted. Jake was scared but he wanted his ball back so he climbed over the fence.

Then he saw a old man. The old man was angry and shouted at him. Then Jake ran away. Then he remembered his ball so he went back and then the old man gave him the ball and said sorry for shouting and then they was friends. Then Jake went home and had his tea.

The next day Jake played football again and he was happy. Then he woke up and it was all a dream. The end.

## What the marker should catch

- Genre: narrative, with a level drop (rushed timeline; dream-sequence cop-out ending).
- Content & Structure around Level 2-3 (events only partly developed, no real climax).
- Style & Accuracy around Level 3 (simple sentences; errors such as 'saturday', 'a old', 'they was').
