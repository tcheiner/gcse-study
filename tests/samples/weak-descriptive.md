# Weak descriptive: drifts into a story, list-like, SPaG errors, dream ending

## Question

Describe an object that has been broken for a long time.

## Answer

The object I am going to describe is a old bike. It is in my garage. It has been broken for a long time.

The bike is red. It is rusty. The chain is broken and the tyres are flat. The seat is ripped and there is stuff coming out of it. It is very dusty and there are spider webs on it. It looks sad. it is really old.

My dad gave me the bike for my birthday when I was seven. I rode it every day after school. One day me and my freind Sam went to the park and I went down a big hill realy fast and then I hit a rock and then I fell off and then the chain broke. I was crying and Sam ran to get my mum and then we went home and my dad said he would fix it but he never did.

Now the bike just sits there in the garage next to the lawnmower and some boxes. Nobody uses it anymore its to small for me now anyway.

Then I woke up and it was all a dream and the bike was fixed.

## What the marker should catch

- Genre: descriptive, with a level drop (the middle turns into a narrative; dream-sequence ending).
- Content & Structure around Level 2-3 (list-like, 'and then' pacing).
- Style & Accuracy around Level 3 (simple vocabulary; errors such as 'a old', 'freind', 'realy', 'its to small', missing capital).
